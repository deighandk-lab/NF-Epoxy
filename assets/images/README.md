# NF Epoxy — Phase 2 Image Specifications

## Purpose

Phase 2 replaces generic/stock-looking visual content with premium, epoxy-specific imagery that matches the visual feel of the current homepage hero.

The client does not currently have project photography. Do not wait for client photos before improving the visual direction. Use this brief as the specification for future generated or sourced images.

## Visual direction

- Premium architectural/editorial photography
- High-end residential and commercial interiors
- Sophisticated, realistic materials and surfaces
- Glossy, polished epoxy/resin surfaces with believable reflections
- Natural architectural lighting, preferably warm daylight or controlled luxury interior lighting
- Deep navy / charcoal / warm neutral environment that complements the NF Epoxy identity
- Clean composition with generous negative space where useful
- Photorealistic texture and material detail
- Subtle depth of field where appropriate
- Professional commercial photography feel
- Aspirational but believable
- No visible AI artefacts

## Hard NOs

- Generic AI-looking epoxy blobs
- Overly saturated neon blue resin
- Unrealistic floating furniture
- Impossible material physics
- Plastic-looking floors
- Excessive reflections
- Fake-looking luxury mansions
- Obviously staged AI showroom scenes
- Generic stock-photo people posing at a camera
- Text, logos, watermarks or fake branding inside generated imagery
- Images that imply a project is NF Epoxy's completed work when it is not

## Required image set

### 1. Homepage hero — premium epoxy interior

Subject: High-end modern home interior featuring a clearly visible premium epoxy/resin floor or large epoxy surface.

Composition: Wide 16:9. Architectural hero shot. Floor/surface should be a major visual element without dominating the entire frame.

Feel: Similar premium architectural mood to the existing homepage hero, but the epoxy material must be visually believable and identifiable.

Lighting: Warm natural daylight entering through large windows, with controlled highlights on the polished surface.

Avoid: Overly futuristic architecture, fantasy interiors, exaggerated reflections.

Suggested filename: hero-epoxy-interior.webp

### 2. Residential flooring

Subject: Elegant residential living space with a realistic seamless epoxy floor.
Composition: Wide 16:9. Show enough room context to communicate the application.
Feel: Premium home renovation / architectural magazine photography.
Suggested filename: application-residential-flooring.webp

### 3. Premium epoxy countertop

Subject: Luxury modern kitchen featuring a high-end epoxy/resin countertop.
Composition: Wide 16:9 or 4:3. Countertop should be clearly visible.
Material: Sophisticated stone/resin appearance; realistic depth and finish.
Suggested filename: application-epoxy-countertop.webp

### 4. Commercial/industrial flooring

Subject: Clean premium commercial or light-industrial environment with a professionally finished epoxy floor.
Composition: Wide 16:9. Show scale and floor surface.
Feel: Professional and credible rather than gritty or warehouse-like.
Suggested filename: application-commercial-flooring.webp

### 5. Hands-on workshop

Subject: Small group learning epoxy techniques around a workbench, with participants actively mixing, pouring or working on their own epoxy projects.
Composition: Wide 16:9, documentary/editorial photography.
Feel: Authentic hands-on training, premium workshop environment.
Important: Participants should look naturally engaged in the work, not posed for a stock photograph.
Suggested filename: workshop-hands-on.webp

### 6. Arts & crafts workshop

Subject: Premium tabletop scene showing realistic epoxy craft projects such as a cheese board, coasters and key chain being made.
Composition: 4:3 or 16:9.
Feel: Craft studio / premium maker workshop.
Suggested filename: workshop-arts-crafts.webp

### 7. Braai plankie workshop

Subject: Premium South African braai-plankie project being crafted using epoxy and wood.
Composition: Wide 16:9 or 4:3.
Feel: Authentic South African lifestyle + premium craftsmanship.
Suggested filename: workshop-braai-plankie.webp

### 8. Flooring workshop

Subject: Participants learning practical epoxy floor preparation/application in a clean training environment.
Composition: Wide 16:9.
Feel: Real hands-on trade training, professionally photographed.
Suggested filename: workshop-flooring.webp

## Image consistency rules

All images should share:
- Similar exposure and contrast
- Similar premium architectural/editorial photography language
- Realistic material physics
- Consistent aspect ratios per placement
- No embedded text
- No artificial logos
- No fake company signage
- No claims that the image represents an actual NF Epoxy project

## Website usage

Until actual NF Epoxy project photography is available:
- Label imagery as illustrative where necessary.
- Do not create a fake portfolio/gallery.
- Do not write captions such as 'Our latest project' unless the image is genuinely NF Epoxy work.
- Use imagery to communicate the type and quality of application, not proof of completed client projects.
- Keep the existing NF Epoxy logo unchanged.
- Preserve the premium visual identity established by the homepage hero.

## Phase 2 implementation order

1. Homepage hero / application imagery
2. Services page imagery
3. Workshops page imagery
4. Products page imagery once product information is supplied
5. Replace illustrative imagery with genuine NF Epoxy project photography whenever the client obtains it

## Acceptance standard

An image is ready for the website only if it passes all of these tests:
- Looks like premium commercial photography at first glance
- Clearly relates to epoxy/resin or the relevant workshop
- Fits the NF Epoxy navy/premium visual identity
- Contains no visible AI artefacts
- Does not make unsupported claims about NF Epoxy's actual work
- Looks coherent beside the existing homepage hero
