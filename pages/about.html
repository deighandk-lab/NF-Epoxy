Established in April 2025, NF Epoxy is based in Kempton Park and serves clients across Gauteng through professional epoxy applications and hands-on workshops.

Who We Are

We don't just teach epoxy — we teach you how to work with it.

NF Epoxy specialises in professional epoxy flooring and countertop applications, as well as practical, hands-on epoxy workshops.

Our workshops are designed around learning by doing. Participants work on their own projects under guidance and supervision, learning correct techniques, preparation methods, mixing and application processes, and practical skills they can confidently use on future projects.

NF Epoxy works with residential, commercial and industrial customers, as well as contractors, sub-contractors and workshop participants.
